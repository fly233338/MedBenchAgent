Ground every target in named annotation fields, assets, regions, or a registered deterministic derivation. Ground-truth computability alone does not make a task feasible: the permitted model input must contain enough information to determine the answer.

Missing evidence is not negative evidence. Do not infer completeness, absence, zero counts, pairing, or comparability from aggregate counts.
